Tribute to Frank Fennell

Many members will have been saddened to hear of the passing of Frank Fennell, who gave an extraordinary amount of time, care and energy to CLTC over many years.

Frank served the club in many roles, including Club Captain, Vice President and committee member. He was always ready to volunteer, whether that meant welcoming new members, helping with matches and activities, organising club events, or quietly getting on with the maintenance jobs that kept the club running smoothly.

His practical skills, generosity and commitment made a lasting difference to the club. Frank played a key part in major projects including the replacement clubhouse and floodlight installations, and many members will also remember his work on Finals Day, club BBQs, social events and the centenary celebrations. He took real pride in the success and sustainability of CLTC, and in seeing both adult and junior tennis thrive.

Above all, Frank will be remembered for his kindness, thoughtfulness and the warm welcome he gave to so many people at the club. Our thanks and condolences go to Jean and all of Frank's family. He will be greatly missed and warmly remembered.

Membership update

The committee has spent a great deal of time reviewing the membership structure for 2026/27, with the aim of keeping the club financially sustainable while also being as fair and thoughtful as possible for members and families in the face of rising costs. Thank you to everyone who has already renewed and for your continued support of the club.

If you have any membership queries or need to follow up on your renewal, please contact the Membership Secretary at cltcmembership@gmail.com.

Membership fees have increased by 5% for 2026/27 to help meet the growing costs of running the club.
Youth membership is now for ages 19-23, based on age on 1 April, creating a clearer step between junior and adult membership.
A new student membership allows members away during term time to play during college or university holidays for £50.
Junior members who are part of a family receive a 40% discount on their membership fee.
The club has retained the family cap for up to two adults and four children under 18.
Youth membership is not included in family discounted rates.

Friendly club reminders

🧹 Clay courts

Please sweep the artificial clay courts after play and clean your shoes before heading into the clubhouse.

🏠 Clubhouse

Please clear up after yourself, wash or put away glasses and mugs, and leave the bar and seating areas as you would like to find them.

🚪 Last to leave

Please turn off lights, close windows, and make sure all doors are properly shut and latched before leaving.

🔐 Court gates

Please close the gates to the hard courts, clay courts and hitting wall when you finish playing. It helps keep the courts secure, protects the playing areas, and reduces the chance of wildlife finding its way in.

🎾 Tuesday social tennis

When Tuesday evening social is busy and a member is waiting, please shorten the set to first to 5 games.

🟡 Tennis balls

The committee has reviewed our ball supplier and noted the feedback about the new balls feeling fuzzier than expected. We have spent time looking into the options and still believe these are the best choice for the club overall. The fuzziness does wear off with play and the balls remain suitable for use, but we will continue to monitor this.

🍺 Bar prices

Beer prices at the bar are increasing by 50p. Thank you for continuing to support the club bar, which helps fund the running of the clubhouse and club activities.

📅 Mid-week members

Mid-week members are very welcome to take part in box leagues and the annual tournament. Please try to arrange these matches during the week where possible. If a weekend match is occasionally needed, the mid-week member should be signed in as a visitor and the usual visitor fee paid. Finals Day is an exception, and no visitor fee will apply for mid-week members playing in a final.

Court booking

Courts are naturally busier in spring and summer, especially with tournament matches, team practice and social tennis all in full swing. Please book through ClubSpark or the ClubSpark Booker app before playing, even if the courts look quiet when you arrive.

Booking helps everyone see court availability clearly and makes it easier for members to plan their tennis. If you are flexible, daytime slots and Sunday afternoons are often quieter.
