Getting started

Finding the right coaching group.

Our coaches will help find the right starting point for your child. Groups are recommended according to age, experience and playing ability, after the coaches have assessed the player.

Contact Chris or Aaron to discuss your child’s experience and arrange the next step. They can advise on a suitable group, availability and coaching costs before you commit.

More ways to play

Junior tennis beyond lessons.

Friends and family

Junior members are encouraged to play outside lessons with friends and family. Family mini-tennis red mornings offer another way to get involved. Ask the coaching team about current activities.

Holiday camps

School-holiday camps welcome members and non-members of all standards, aged 4–15. Contact Chris or Aaron for dates, availability, costs and booking arrangements.

Matchplay and teams

Junior tournaments and team opportunities give children the chance to try competitive tennis. The coaching team can recommend suitable events as your child develops.

Junior membership

2026/27 annual membership.

Membership runs from 1 April to 31 March. Juniors need club membership to take part in regular weekly lessons. Speak to a coach first to discuss a suitable group and the membership arrangements.

Membership and coaching are different costs. The prices below are for club membership, not lessons or holiday camps. Please ask the coaches about coaching and camp charges.

Playing as a family?

Family membership is £545 a year for up to 2 adults and 4 children. Parents who would like to play with their children can also ask the membership secretary about associate membership.
