Adult coaching at CLTC

Adult tennis coaching.

Individual lessons and group coaching for adults of all abilities. Whether you’re new to tennis, returning after a break or looking to improve, Chris and Aaron can help you find a suitable starting point.

Coaching fees are additional to club membership.

Talk to our coaches ↓ Explore group coaching ↓

Individual coaching

Lessons arranged around your game.

One-to-one coaching gives you time to work on your own goals, whether that’s learning the basics, improving a particular shot or building confidence for matches.

Contact Chris or Aaron to discuss what you’d like to work on. They can explain availability, lesson arrangements and costs.

Group coaching

Monday and Wednesday evenings.

Group sessions offer a way to practise with other players and develop your game with a coach. Speak to Chris or Aaron about your experience and ability so they can recommend a suitable group.
