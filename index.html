Who we are

A friendly club with plenty of ways to play.

CLTC is a community tennis club in Carshalton Beeches. Members range from complete beginners and social players through to team players, juniors and families.

We aim to make it easy to get involved, whether you want a regular social game, structured coaching, competitive matches, junior tennis, table tennis or simply a local club with a welcoming clubhouse.

What we do

Tennis, coaching, teams and table tennis.

Use the club in the way that suits you: casual play, social sessions, coaching, competitive teams, box leagues, junior sessions or table tennis.

Social tennis Regular turn-up-and-play sessions for friendly doubles and meeting other members. Coaching Group and individual coaching for adults and juniors with the club coaching team. Junior tennis Junior coaching, holiday camps, tournaments and family-friendly tennis. Team tennis and box leagues Three men's teams, three women's teams, mixed team tennis, box leagues and Thursday team practice. Table tennis Table tennis in the clubhouse, including social play and teams. Clubhouse and socials A licensed bar, social events through the year and clubhouse hire for members.

Competitive tennis

Teams, box leagues and matchplay.

CLTC has three men's teams and three women's teams competing in Surrey League competitions, alongside mixed team tennis.

Our open singles, women's singles and mixed doubles box leagues offer regular matches with members of a similar standard. There are boxes for a range of levels, welcoming players of different ages and abilities.

Team practice takes place on Thursday evenings and selection is organised by the team captains.

Adult teams
Three men's teams, three women's teams and a mixed team give competitive players regular match opportunities.
Box leagues
Open singles, women's singles and mixed doubles. Play people at your level and arrange matches around your schedule.
Junior pathway
Junior players can progress into matchplay and team tennis through the coaching programme, with competitive opportunities confirmed by the coaching team.
Getting involved
For box leagues, see how to join the WhatsApp community. For team tennis, come to Thursday team practice or speak to the relevant team captain.
